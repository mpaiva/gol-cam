## What shipped

### `4ae5e9c` /test-sound PWM beep probe
Endpoint to sweep candidate GPIOs at a chosen frequency/duration so
the operator can identify which pin is wired to the speaker on a
fresh HMI without reflashing.

### `9a1525f` SD card + IP label + goal jingle
Three additions:
- **SD card support** on the CrowPanel's SPI bus (CS=10 MOSI=11 SCK=12
  MISO=13). New `/sd-ls?dir=&ext=` endpoint enumerates files. The
  `/gol-sound` folder on .88's SD contains `gol-brasil.mp3` (213 KB).
- **Self-IP label** rendered in tiny grey text at the bottom-right of
  the scoreboard pill — two physically-identical HMIs can be told
  apart at a glance.
- **Goal celebration jingle** ("gol-gol-gol!" + rising E6→G6→C7
  fanfare) via LEDC PWM on **pin 17** (the speaker GPIO identified
  by the /test-sound sweep). Fires automatically on `/goal` pushes
  from cameras and on manual `/a+` `/b+` increments. POST `/play` to
  trigger manually.

### `e22f7de`, `91ba023`, `f50a71e` MP3 attempt — definitively ruled out
The buzzer on the CrowPanel DIS07050H is a tone-only piezo: it
resonates at the LEDC carrier frequency itself, not at content
sigma-modulated into it. Tested three different ways, all silent:
1. `arduino-audio-tools` `PWMAudioOutput` at 22 kHz square modulation.
2. Hand-rolled 80 kHz LEDC carrier + duty modulation at 22 kHz sample
   rate.
3. Full MP3 decode of `gol-brasil.mp3` (libhelix) → 14 s of decoded
   PCM pushed to `PWMAudioOutput`. The decode ran cleanly to
   completion, the speaker stayed silent.

Final code drops the audio-tools + libhelix dependencies and the
`/play-mp3` endpoint, leaves the MP3 file on the SD as reference,
documents the conclusion + the I²S-amp upgrade path (MAX98357 +
speaker on pins 17/18/38) inline.

## Key non-obvious findings (for future-you)

- **The "safe pins" list in the old comment block was wrong for I²S**:
  17/18/38/42 each broke the RGB display via GDMA contention when an
  I²S driver claimed them, even though they were listed as
  GPIO-toggle-safe. LEDC PWM does not have this problem; use LEDC for
  anything audio-related on this board.
- **Pin 10/11/12/13 are the SD bus.** That's why the speaker probe on
  those pins was silent — they aren't free, they're routed to the
  microSD slot. The DFR1154 `pins.h` documentation happens to match
  the CrowPanel SD pinout.
- **Speaker connection is the recurring flaky wire** — at least four
  times during the session a "no sound" answer was a loose JST wire
  on .88 or .89, not a software bug. When debugging audio, reseat
  the wire BEFORE assuming a code regression.
- **The CH340 USB-serial on the CrowPanel is unreliable on macOS.**
  Cables matter (some are charge-only); the kext occasionally
  enters a stuck termios state that survives reconnect; unplug for
  30+ seconds to fully release, or hold BOOT while plugging in to
  jump straight into ROM download mode.
